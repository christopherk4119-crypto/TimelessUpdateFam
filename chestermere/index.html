Chestermere housing & project types

What Chestermere's layout means for renovation projects

01

Walkout basements are common near the lake

Many homes closer to Chestermere Lake are built with walkout basements, which affects window placement, natural light, and how a basement development project is laid out compared to a standard below-grade basement.

02

Newer housing stock means basement development, not gut renovations

Since most of Chestermere was built more recently than Calgary's inner city, basement finishing is typically about developing unfinished space rather than replacing old materials.

03

Chestermere issues its own building permits

The City of Chestermere manages its own permitting process, separate from Calgary. We handle this directly as part of every project requiring a permit.
