Why homeowners convert

Why a tub-to-shower conversion makes sense

Most households we talk to have one bathtub in the house they actually use, and every other tub sits empty. Converting an unused tub to a walk-in shower gets you a bigger, more usable shower without touching the rest of the bathroom.

01

More usable space, same footprint

A shower fills the same floor space a tub occupied, but the interior usable area is larger since there's no tub wall cutting into it.

02

Curb height is a real decision

A standard curb, a low-profile curb, or a fully curbless entry each have different waterproofing and floor-slope requirements — worth deciding before the quote, not during construction.

03

Waterproofing is what actually prevents leaks

A tile job looks the same whether or not the membrane underneath was done properly — the difference shows up as a leak into the ceiling below a year or two later. We waterproof every conversion to the same standard as a full renovation.

Before your consultation

A few things worth thinking about first

04

Resale isn't always a reason to keep one tub in the house

Some buyers look for at least one tub, but plenty of Calgary listings sell fine with all showers — if you have more than one bathroom, converting the one you actually use daily is usually the better call.

05

The ceiling below matters as much as the shower above

If there's finished space below the bathroom, we check the ceiling access and plan the waterproofing and any inspection points accordingly before work starts.

06

Matching existing tile elsewhere in the home

If the rest of your bathroom is staying as-is, we can often source tile that complements what's already there rather than forcing a full re-tile of walls that don't need it.

Common questions

Tub-to-shower conversion FAQs

Do you offer a curbless (zero-threshold) shower option?

Yes. A curbless entry requires the subfloor to be built down or the surrounding floor built up slightly so water drains correctly — we assess whether your existing floor structure supports this during the free consultation.

Can you convert a tub to a shower without moving the drain?

In most cases yes, since the shower base can be sized to use the existing tub drain location. Moving the drain is sometimes worth it for a better layout, but it adds plumbing work and cost.

How long does a tub-to-shower conversion take?

Most conversions take about 1–2 weeks, faster than a full bathroom renovation since the vanity, toilet, and layout typically stay in place.
