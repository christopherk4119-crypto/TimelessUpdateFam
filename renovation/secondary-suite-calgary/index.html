Common questions

Secondary suite FAQs

Does Timeless Interiors pull the building permit, or does the homeowner?

We pull the building permit and manage the inspection process as part of every secondary suite project.

Does Timeless Interiors handle the Secondary Suite Registry entry after inspections?

Yes. Once your suite passes its final inspections, we help you complete the Secondary Suite Registry entry so it's fully legal to rent.

How much does a secondary suite cost in Calgary?

Cost depends on layout, whether an egress window needs to be added, and finish level. You'll receive a fully itemized, fixed-price quote after a free in-home consultation.
