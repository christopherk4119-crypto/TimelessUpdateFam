Space-saving decisions

What actually makes a small bathroom feel bigger

These are the layout choices that make the biggest difference in a powder room or small 3-piece bathroom.

01

A floating vanity opens up the floor

Wall-mounted vanities and toilets show more floor space underneath, which reads as a bigger room even though the footprint hasn't changed.

02

A pocket door can reclaim real floor space

A standard swing door needs clearance a small bathroom often can't spare — converting to a pocket door frees up that arc entirely.

03

Larger tile, fewer grout lines

Large-format tile on the floor and lower walls reduces the number of visible grout lines, which makes a small room read as more continuous.

Before your consultation

A few things worth thinking about first

04

Lighting placement matters more in a small room

A single overhead fixture can leave a small bathroom feeling flat — a combination of a vanity light and a recessed ceiling light usually reads as brighter and larger without adding floor-space demands.

05

Recessed storage beats surface-mounted storage

A recessed medicine cabinet or a niche built into the wall keeps storage available without eating into the usable floor space a surface-mounted cabinet would take up.

06

A single large mirror reads bigger than two small ones

One full-width mirror above the vanity reflects more light and reads as a larger wall than two separate framed mirrors, at a similar cost.

Common questions

Small bathroom renovation FAQs

Can a small bathroom really fit a walk-in shower?

Often yes — a neo-angle or corner shower can fit spaces a rectangular shower can't, and we can show you options during your free consultation.

Is a pocket door always possible?

It depends on what's inside the adjacent wall — plumbing or electrical can sometimes block a pocket door conversion, which we check before quoting.

Do small bathrooms cost less to renovate?

Generally yes, since there's less floor area to tile and fewer fixtures, but layout changes (like a pocket door or wall-hung fixtures) can offset some of that savings.
