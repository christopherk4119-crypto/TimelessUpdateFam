A below-grade bathroom has a few extra requirements

Adding a bathroom to a basement usually means dealing with the fact that the drain sits below the main sewer line — the details below are what a basement bathroom needs that a main-floor bathroom doesn't.

  • Sewage ejector pump (where required)
  • Plumbing rough-in below grade
  • Ventilation & exhaust fan sizing
  • Ceiling height assessment
  • Building permit application
  • Tile & fixture installation
  • Fixed-price quote
  • Two-year craftsmanship warranty

What's different below grade

Why a basement bathroom isn't just a smaller version of an upstairs one

Three things come up in almost every basement bathroom project that don't apply upstairs.

01

The drain is usually below the main sewer line

Gravity can't move waste uphill to the sewer line, so a sewage ejector pump is often required to pump it up and out — a real cost most homeowners don't budget for upfront.

02

Ceiling height affects fixture placement

Ductwork and structural beams sometimes force a toilet or shower to a different spot than you'd first assume — we check this before quoting, not after demo starts.

03

Ventilation still matters, even without a window

A basement bathroom needs a properly sized exhaust fan vented to the exterior — this is a code requirement, not an optional upgrade.

Before your consultation

A few things worth thinking about first

04

Combining it with a basement finishing or secondary suite project

A basement bathroom is most cost-effective when it's planned alongside a larger basement finishing or secondary suite project, since the plumbing rough-in and permit process overlap.

05

Noise from the ejector pump is manageable

A sewage ejector pump does make some operating noise — locating it in a small enclosed utility closet with sound-dampening keeps it from being noticeable in daily use.

06

Radiant floor heat is worth considering below grade

Basement floors run colder than upstairs floors year-round, which makes in-floor heating a upgrade many homeowners find worth the added cost specifically in a basement bathroom.

Common questions

Basement bathroom FAQs

Do all basement bathrooms need a sewage ejector pump?

Not all — it depends on where your basement's drain sits relative to the main sewer line. We check this during the free consultation before quoting.

Do I need a permit to add a bathroom to my basement?

Yes, the City of Calgary requires a building permit for basement plumbing and bathroom additions. We handle the permitting process as part of every project.

Can a basement bathroom be added as part of a larger basement finishing project?

Yes — a bathroom is commonly added as part of a full basement finishing or secondary suite project, and can also be done as its own standalone project.
