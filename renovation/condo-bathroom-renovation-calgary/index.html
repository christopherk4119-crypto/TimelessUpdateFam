What's different in a condo

What a condo board expects before you start

Most condo corporations require an application and proof of insurance before any renovation work begins — these are the pieces that come up most often.

01

Board approval usually comes first

Most condo boards want a description of the work and a certificate of insurance before approving your renovation — we can supply the documentation your board asks for.

02

Work hours are usually restricted

Buildings commonly limit renovation noise to weekday daytime hours — we plan the schedule around your building's specific bylaw.

03

Elevators and hallways may need protection

Moving materials and debris through shared hallways and elevators often requires protective coverings the building manager will want confirmed in advance.

Before your consultation

A few things worth thinking about first

04

Start the board application early

Condo board approval timelines vary widely by building — submitting your application as soon as you have a scope in mind avoids it becoming the longest step in the whole project.

05

Sound transmission is worth planning for

Tile and plumbing changes can transmit noise to neighbouring units — we can discuss sound-dampening underlayment options during your consultation if this is a concern in your building.

06

Shutoff valve location should be confirmed early

Some older condo buildings have shared shutoff valves rather than in-suite ones — confirming this with your building manager before demo avoids delays mid-project.
