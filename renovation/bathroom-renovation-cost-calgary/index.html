Cost Factors

What Drives Bathroom Renovation Cost in Calgary

No two bathrooms cost the same to renovate — here's what actually moves the number, so your free consultation starts from an informed place.

What's included

Why we don't quote a number before seeing your bathroom

The five biggest factors

What actually moves the number

01

Scope: refresh vs. full gut-to-studs

Replacing fixtures and finishes in the same layout costs far less than tearing a bathroom down to the studs and rebuilding it, since a gut renovation touches framing, plumbing, and electrical that a cosmetic refresh never opens up.

02

Whether plumbing or walls move

A like-for-like fixture swap reuses the existing rough-in. Relocating a toilet, shower, or sink — or removing a wall to change the layout — adds plumbing, framing, and sometimes structural work a simple swap doesn't need.

03

Tile and fixture grade

A builder-grade vanity and ceramic tile costs meaningfully less than a floating vanity, natural stone, or a curbless walk-in shower with a linear drain. Neither choice is wrong — it depends on what the space is worth to you.

04

Basement-specific plumbing needs

If you're adding a bathroom below grade, the drain is often below the main sewer line, which can require a sewage ejector pump — a real added cost that doesn't apply to a main-floor bathroom.

05

Permits and inspections

Most bathroom renovations involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes require a City of Calgary permit. We handle this process for every project, and it's built into your fixed-price quote rather than billed separately.

Why fixed-price

Why we quote a fixed price instead of an hourly rate

An hourly or "cost-plus" quote puts the risk of a longer-than-expected job on you. A fixed-price quote puts that risk on us — we define the full scope during your free in-home consultation, and the number we hand you within 48 hours is the number you pay, regardless of how long the work actually takes to do properly.

A note on timelines

Timeline affects cost too

A standard bathroom renovation typically takes 2–4 weeks from demolition to final walkthrough, while a full ensuite rebuild with layout changes can run 4–6 weeks. Rushing a timeline usually means paying for overtime labour or expedited material shipping — building in a realistic schedule during your consultation is one of the simplest ways to keep the quote accurate. We provide a realistic timeline as part of every fixed-price quote, so there are no surprises once work begins.

Staying on budget

What can still change the number after the quote

A fixed-price quote covers the scope we agree on together — but a couple of things can still come up once walls are open, and it's worth knowing about them upfront rather than being surprised.

01

Hidden damage behind existing walls

Water damage, mold, or outdated wiring found once demo starts isn't something we can quote sight-unseen — if we find it, we stop, show you exactly what we found, and quote the additional work separately before proceeding.

02

Upgrades you decide on mid-project

If you see the space demolished and decide you'd rather upgrade the tile or add a feature that wasn't in the original scope, that's a change order — a separate, itemized addition to the original quote, not a renegotiation of it.

03

What does NOT change: the scope you already agreed to

Everything covered in your original itemized quote stays at the original price, regardless of how long it actually takes us to complete it properly. That's the entire point of a fixed-price quote instead of an hourly one.

Common questions

Bathroom renovation cost FAQs

Why won't you give me a price range without seeing my bathroom?

Because the factors above can each swing the number significantly on their own, a generic range would be more likely to mislead than help. A free in-home consultation lets us quote your specific bathroom accurately.

Does moving my bathroom's layout always cost significantly more?

It depends on what's moving — relocating a toilet a few feet is a smaller change than moving a shower to the opposite wall. We can tell you the specific impact during your consultation.

Is the fixed-price quote final, or can it change during the project?

The quote covers the scope we agree on together. If you decide to add something new mid-project — like upgrading tile after demo starts — that's a change order we'll quote separately; the original scope stays at the original price.
