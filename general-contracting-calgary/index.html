Common questions

General contracting FAQs

What does a general contractor do?

A general contractor manages your entire renovation or addition from start to finish — coordinating trades, permits, scheduling, and quality control so you have a single point of contact.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes, permitting and inspection scheduling are included in every general contracting project we manage.

Can you manage a whole-home renovation while we live in the house?

In many cases yes — we plan phasing and scheduling around your household wherever possible and discuss logistics during your free consultation.

What's the difference between hiring a general contractor and hiring trades myself?

A general contractor takes on the coordination, scheduling, and accountability for the whole project, so you don't have to manage multiple trades, timelines, and hand-offs yourself.
