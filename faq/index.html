FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about pricing, permits, timelines, and service area for Calgary bathroom renovation, basement finishing, and general contracting projects.

How much does a home renovation cost in Calgary?

It depends on scope, but most bathroom renovations range from $12,000–$30,000 and basement finishing typically runs $35,000–$70,000+ depending on size and finishes. We provide a fully itemized, fixed-price quote after a free in-home consultation so you know the exact cost before work begins.

Is Timeless Interiors licensed and insured in Alberta?

Yes. All work is performed by licensed tradespeople and covered by Alberta contractor insurance, and every project includes a two-year craftsmanship warranty on labour and finishes.

What areas around Calgary do you serve?

We serve Calgary and the surrounding communities of Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere, and Okotoks for bathroom renovation, basement finishing, and general contracting.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes — every project starts with a free, no-obligation in-home consultation where we assess scope and discuss your vision.

How do your fixed-price quotes work?

After your free consultation, we lock in scope and budget and send a fully itemized, fixed-price quote within 48 hours. That quote is your price — no hidden costs or surprise change orders for the scope we agreed on.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A standard bathroom renovation typically takes 2–4 weeks from demolition to final walkthrough, depending on scope.

Do I need a permit to renovate my bathroom in Calgary?

Most bathroom renovations involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes require a City of Calgary permit. We handle the permitting process for you as part of every project.

How much does basement finishing cost in Calgary?

Basement finishing typically ranges from $35,000–$70,000+ depending on size, layout complexity, and whether a bathroom or secondary suite is included.

Can you build a legal secondary suite in my basement?

Yes, we build basement developments to current secondary suite requirements, including egress windows, ceiling height, and separate entrances where required.

What does a general contractor do?

A general contractor manages your entire renovation or addition from start to finish — coordinating trades, permits, scheduling, and quality control so you have a single point of contact.

Can you manage a whole-home renovation while we live in the house?

In many cases yes — we plan phasing and scheduling around your household wherever possible and discuss logistics during your free consultation.

What does your two-year warranty cover?

Our two-year craftsmanship warranty covers labour and finishes on every project. If something isn't right within two years of completion, we make it right at no cost to you.
