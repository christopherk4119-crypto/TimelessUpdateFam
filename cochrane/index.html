Why Cochrane homeowners choose us

Cochrane's mix of older and newer homes needs different approaches

Cochrane has grown fast in the last two decades, but it also has a genuinely old townsite core — which means the renovation needs vary more from home to home than in a purely new-build community.

Cochrane housing & project types

What Cochrane's housing mix means for renovation projects

01

Older Cochrane homes often need a full gut renovation

Homes in the original townsite and older established areas can have galvanized plumbing and outdated electrical — a bathroom renovation in these homes is often a chance to address both at once, since the walls are already open.

02

Newer communities need basement development

Areas like Sunset Ridge, Heritage Hills, and Riversong were built more recently and commonly include unfinished basements ready for development into livable space.

03

Cochrane issues its own building permits

The Town of Cochrane handles its own permitting, separate from Calgary and separate from Rocky View County. We manage this process directly as part of every project that requires it.

Common questions

Cochrane renovation FAQs

Do you work in both older and newer parts of Cochrane?

Yes — from the historic townsite to newer communities like Sunset Ridge and Heritage Hills.

Do older Cochrane homes need different renovation work?

Often, yes. Older homes can have outdated plumbing or electrical behind the walls, which we address as part of a bathroom or basement renovation when the walls are already open.

Does Cochrane or Rocky View County issue the building permit for my project?

It depends on your specific address — the Town of Cochrane and Rocky View County are separate jurisdictions. We confirm which applies during your free consultation and handle the permit application.
