Common questions

Bathroom renovation FAQs

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Calgary?

Most bathroom renovations range from $12,000–$30,000 depending on size, fixtures, and finishes. You'll receive an itemized, fixed-price quote after a free in-home consultation.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A standard bathroom renovation typically takes 2–4 weeks from demolition to final walkthrough, depending on scope.

Do I need a permit to renovate my bathroom in Calgary?

Most bathroom renovations involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes require a City of Calgary permit. We handle the permitting process for you as part of every project.

Can you match my existing tile or fixtures?

In most cases, yes — we source tile and fixtures to match or complement your home's existing style, and can advise on options during your free consultation.
