Planning your project

What to consider before you paint

A few practical things worth thinking about before your consultation.

01

Exterior painting is seasonal in Calgary

Exterior paint needs a minimum surface and air temperature to cure properly, which generally limits exterior projects to the warmer months — worth planning ahead if you're booking for spring or fall.

02

Popcorn ceiling removal in older homes

For homes built before 1990, asbestos testing is recommended before any ceiling texture is disturbed — ask us about this during your free consultation.

03

Finish level changes the cost and the process

A standard finish, a knockdown texture, and a level 5 smooth finish each involve different amounts of prep work — deciding on finish level upfront keeps your quote accurate.

Common questions

Painting FAQs

Does Timeless Interiors offer interior painting only, or exterior painting as well?

Both. We paint interior walls, ceilings, trim, and doors, as well as exterior surfaces — including full surface prep and priming so the finish holds up.

Does Timeless Interiors remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes — we remove textured "popcorn" ceilings and refinish to a smooth knockdown texture or a level 5 finish. For homes built before 1990, asbestos testing is recommended before any ceiling texture is disturbed; ask us about this during your free consultation.

Can painting be bundled with a bathroom, basement, or general contracting project?

Yes. Painting is often added on to a larger renovation so it's handled by the same crew and project timeline, with one point of contact from start to finish.
