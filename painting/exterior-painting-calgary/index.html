Planning your project

What to consider before you paint outdoors

A few practical things worth thinking about before your consultation.

01

Exterior painting is seasonal in Calgary

Exterior paint and stain need a minimum surface and air temperature to cure properly, which generally limits exterior projects to the warmer months — worth planning ahead if you're booking for spring or fall.

02

Staining vs. painting a deck

Staining lets the wood grain show through and needs reapplication more often; paint gives full opaque coverage and can hide older or mismatched boards. Which makes sense depends on your deck's condition and the look you want.

03

Brick needs the right prep and primer

Painted brick looks great, but it's close to a one-way decision — proper cleaning and a masonry-appropriate primer up front are what keep it from peeling later.

Common questions

Exterior painting FAQs

What time of year can exterior painting be done in Calgary?

Exterior paint and stain need a minimum surface and air temperature to cure properly, which generally limits exterior projects to the warmer months — typically late spring through early fall. Booking ahead for spring or fall is worth planning around, since good-weather windows fill up.

Do you paint or stain decks?

Yes — both. Staining lets more of the wood grain show through, while paint gives full, opaque colour coverage and can hide older or mismatched boards. We can walk through both options during your free consultation.

Can you paint brick?

Yes. Brick painting requires proper cleaning and a masonry-appropriate primer before finish coats, so the paint bonds correctly and doesn't peel.
