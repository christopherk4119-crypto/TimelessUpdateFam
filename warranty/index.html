Warranty

Our 2-Year Craftsmanship Warranty

Every Timeless Interiors project — bathroom, basement, secondary suite, painting, or general contracting — is backed by a two-year warranty on labour and finishes.

What's covered

Labour and finishes, for two years

If something we installed or finished fails because of how it was built — not general wear, and not damage from something else entirely — we come back and fix it at no cost to you.

01

Two years from completion

The warranty period runs for two years from your project's final walkthrough — the point where we agree the work is done.

02

Covers our labour and finishing work

Tile, paint, trim, fixtures, and finishing details we installed — if the workmanship fails, we return and correct it.

03

Backed by licensed, insured work

Every project is performed by licensed tradespeople and covered by Alberta contractor insurance from day one.

04

How to make a claim

Call or email us with a description (and photos, if you have them) of the issue, and we'll arrange a time to take a look.
