How Much Does Basement Finishing Cost in Calgary? 2026 Price Guide

Basement finishing is one of the best ways to add livable square footage to a Calgary home without the cost of an addition — but the price range is wide, and it's driven by a handful of specific factors. Here's what actually moves the number.

Typical cost range in Calgary

Most basement finishing projects in Calgary run $35,000–$70,000+, depending on size, layout, and finish level. A simple open rec room finish sits at the lower end; adding a bathroom, wet bar, or secondary suite pushes it higher.

What drives the cost

Size and layout complexity

More square footage means more framing, drywall, and flooring — but layout matters too. A basement with several small rooms costs more per square foot than one large open space, because of the extra framing and doors involved.

Permits and code requirements

The City of Calgary requires a building permit for basement development. If your basement doesn't already have an egress window meeting current code, adding one is a common and necessary cost for any bedroom being finished below grade.

Bathrooms and wet bars

Adding plumbing below grade — for a bathroom, wet bar, or secondary suite kitchen — is one of the bigger single cost drivers, since it involves rough-in plumbing, venting, and often a sump or ejector pump.

Secondary suite requirements

If the goal is a legal secondary suite, the project needs to meet additional requirements: a separate entrance, proper ceiling height, fire separation, and egress windows in every bedroom. These requirements add cost but are necessary for the suite to be legal and insurable.

Finish level

Builder-grade laminate flooring and basic lighting cost less than engineered hardwood, pot lights on dimmers, and custom built-ins. This is usually where homeowners have the most room to adjust budget up or down.
